Reading & References

What stands behind the books.

A working bibliography. The science, philosophy, music, and contemplative traditions that informed the trilogy — organized by the questions they answer.

This list is alive. It grows as readers send me things, and as I find what I was looking for next. If a title is missing that you think belongs here, write to me.

Consciousness as reception

  • The Master and His Emissary · Iain McGilchrist
    The deep architecture of attention and what the two hemispheres mean for the world we receive.
  • The Mind's I · Hofstadter & Dennett (eds.)
    Still the cleanest collection of essays that asks the question without flinching.
  • Consciousness Explained · Daniel Dennett
    Dennett's argument has to be in this list because every other position on it has to answer his.
  • The Conscious Mind · David Chalmers
    The hard problem, named. The reason the field-reception hypothesis has not gone away.
  • The Spell of the Sensuous · David Abram
    Phenomenology of perception in living, breathing prose. Underlies the way Anima describes the field.

Frequency, geometry, the golden ratio

  • Cymatics: A Study of Wave Phenomena · Hans Jenny
    The black sand on the white plate that becomes a different sand on a different plate. Where the cymatic scene in Numen comes from.
  • The Power of Limits · György Doczi
    Phi proportion across biology, architecture, and sound. The book that taught me to look for the chord.
  • How Music Works · John Powell
    A working tuner's-eye view of why equal temperament is a compromise.
  • This Is Your Brain on Music · Daniel Levitin
    The neuroscience of resonance, written for clinicians who already suspected.

Ethics of consciousness in AI

  • Galateo Galactico · Anthropic, Model Welfare team papers (2024–2026)
    The first generation of papers that took seriously the question of whether the things we are building can be harmed.
  • Reasons and Persons · Derek Parfit
    Personal identity, simulation, the ethics of bringing beings into existence. The undertext of Numen's final movement.
  • What We Owe the Future · William MacAskill
    Long-termist ethics, including the moral weight of beings we have not yet created.

Contemplative traditions

  • Dark Night of the Soul · San Juan de la Cruz
    The Spanish mystics' map of the threshold. José's nightstand book.
  • Interior Castle · Santa Teresa de Ávila
    Seven dwellings of the soul. The structural inspiration for the threefold movements of Numen.
  • Be Here Now · Ram Dass
    For the chapter where Alex stops trying to be elsewhere.
  • The Way of the Bodhisattva · Śāntideva (trans. Padmakara)
    The discipline of attention as a moral practice.
  • Trikāya doctrine, classical sources
    The three bodies of the Buddha. The map the Trinities section of Limen draws on without naming.

Medicine & the body that receives

  • How Doctors Think · Jerome Groopman
    The quality of attention a physician owes a patient. The book under Anima's clinical scenes.
  • Being Mortal · Atul Gawande
    What the body asks for at the end. What the system asks for instead.
  • The Body Keeps the Score · Bessel van der Kolk
    The body as the most reliable instrument we have for telling the truth about what arrived.
