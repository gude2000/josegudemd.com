Novels · 2026

Fiction at the edge of the Consciousness Field.

Four novels about the origin and nature of consciousness, artificial intelligence, philosophy of mind, voluntarism, frequency, family, dogs, love, and freedom.

A series and a stand-alone — written between hospital shifts in Boise, Idaho, by a physician who got tired of pretending the body was only the body.

The Field Trilogy · read about the series →

Anima, Numen, and Limen form a trilogy about what consciousness is and what it costs to take it seriously — a novel that asks the question, a novel that follows the implications, and a companion volume that lays out the science, philosophy, and contemplative testimony underneath the fiction. Fragile Light is a stand-alone novel.

What the evidence shows so far →  A synthesis of the physics, biology, and computation behind the trilogy's thesis — Bell, the Planck floor, ER=EPR, the simulation argument, the field-cosmology programs — woven into a single coherent argument.
