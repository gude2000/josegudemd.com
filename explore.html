The interactive pages

Explore

Three pages on this site translate the trilogy's central claims into something you can manipulate. The reader stops reading about φ and starts dragging it. Stops reading about the φ-tuned C and starts hearing it. Stops reading about the convergence and starts seeing it laid out across three parallel tracks. Each page is short, self-contained, and best read alongside its companion explainer.

All three work in any modern browser, with no installation. The tunings page benefits from headphones; the spiral and the timeline work on any screen.

Geometry · φ

The φ-spiral, interactive

A slider that morphs the growth rate of a logarithmic spiral. Snap to φ to see the only rate that nests Fibonacci squares without remainder. Toggle to phyllotaxis to see what 137.5° does to seed packing — the geometry the trilogy's antenna metaphor rests on.

Tunings — an audio comparison

Five buttons. Each plays the note C tuned a different way: 12-TET A=440, 12-TET A=432, just intonation at C=264, the φ-tuned C=266.67 Hz the trilogy uses, and the φ-interval itself. Pure sine tones generated live in your browser. The frequency claim made audible.

The paradigm-shift timeline

A century of evidence on three parallel tracks — physics, consciousness science, inflection points — from Planck (1900) to D'Ariano-Faggin (2024). Click any entry for the explainer. The pre-1900 banner names the contemplative inheritance the modern story rests on.

The Webb fractal

The recursive triangle structure at the heart of Numen, rendered in 3-D. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, toggle depth to see the self-similar nesting. The visual analog of the trilogy's claim that the same pattern appears at every scale.
