Print one sheet = ten cards. US Letter, 2 columns × 5 rows. Press Cmd-P (or Ctrl-P), confirm the paper size is Letter (8.5 × 11 in), set scale to 100 % (not "Fit to page"), and uncheck Headers and footers. Adjacent cards share their cut lines — one horizontal cut separates two rows, one vertical cut separates the two columns. The faint dashed border on each card is the cut guide; it prints barely visible and disappears once trimmed.
